07 — Product Specification

Product Specification

Technical parameters and performance details of the formulation.

Technical Specifications

Active Ingredients

Ethanol (60%–85% v/v) or Isopropyl Alcohol (60%–75% v/v)

Additional Ingredients

Hydrogen Peroxide (approx. 0.125%–3%), Glycerol/Glycerin (humectant), Purified/Distilled Water

Physical Form

Clear gel or free-flowing liquid rub

pH Range

6.5 to 8.5

Color & Odor

Clear/colorless (unless safe colorants added); characteristic alcohol odor with optional mild floral, citrus, or herbal fragrance

Shelf Life

1 to 2 years from the date of manufacture

Key Performance & Safety Features

Efficacy

Rapidly reduces micro-organisms with a 99.9% to 99.99% germ kill rate

Application

Waterless, fast-drying, and non-sticky formulation

Packaging

Leak-proof plastic bottles, pump dispensers, or bulk canisters — from 50ml pocket sizes up to 500ml and 5-liter containers

Storage Precautions

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and open flame; keep tightly closed and out of reach of children
